📱 Install on iPhone / iPad

Creator Hub installs on iPhone as a home-screen app (PWA). Apple does not allow non-App-Store .ipa installers on a normal iPhone, so this is the official, Apple-approved way to install it. It gets its own icon, opens full-screen with no browser bars, and works offline after the first open.

Before you start

The app must be opened from a web address over https:// (not a file). Host the Website folder on any free static host (GitHub Pages, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel — all free), then open that link on the iPhone. One-time setup; after that the app works offline.

Steps on the iPhone

1
Open the app’s web link in Safari (must be Safari — Chrome on iOS can’t add to Home Screen).
2
Tap the Share button (the square with an up-arrow) at the bottom of Safari.
3
Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
4
Tap Add in the top-right. The Creator Hub icon appears on the Home Screen.
5
Open it once while online so it caches. After that it launches full-screen and works offline.
Fastest free hosting: go to app.netlify.com/drop on a computer, drag in the www folder, and Netlify gives you an https:// link instantly. Open that link on the iPhone and follow the steps above.
Why not a real .ipa installer? A sideloadable iPhone .ipa requires a Mac with Xcode, an Apple Developer account, and Apple code-signing — and even then it must be installed through the App Store, TestFlight, or an enterprise/MDM profile. None of that can be produced offline here. The Home-Screen (PWA) install above is the real, supported alternative and needs no Mac, no account, and no fees.

Android users: install the signed Creator-Hub-App-Release.apk directly — no account needed.
